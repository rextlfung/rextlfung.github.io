Hi! My name is Rex Fung and I'm a 5th year PhD Candidate advised by Dr. Jon-Fredrik Nielsen and Dr. Douglas Noll at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. My research is on random sampling design and low-rank optimization methods for the acquisition and reconstruction of high-resolution fMRI. Outside of school, I'm passionate about sports such as volleyball and rock climbing, with a particular interest in learning the complex biomechanics involved.
